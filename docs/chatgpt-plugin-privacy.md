# Privacy Policy for Workflow Sprint 5 Plugins

Effective date: October 1, 2026

This policy applies to the following ChatGPT plugins published by Stefano Rigante: Invoice Table Extractor, Bank Reconciliation, PO Invoice Match, Timesheet to Invoice, and CSV Cleaner & Validator.

## Data processed

These plugins may process files, text, and structured data that a user chooses to provide or authorize for a requested workflow. Depending on the task, that content can include invoices, bank or ledger transactions, purchase orders, goods receipts, timesheets, CSV files, names, contact or business information, financial transaction details, and other information contained in those documents.

## Purpose

The plugins use the provided content only to perform the workflow requested by the user, such as extraction, reconciliation, matching, validation, cleaning, calculation, or export.

## Developer-operated collection and sharing

These plugins are skills-only packages and do not connect to a developer-operated backend or MCP service. The developer does not receive, collect, sell, rent, or share user prompts, uploaded files, or generated outputs through a developer-operated server, and the plugins contain no developer-operated analytics or telemetry.

OpenAI may process content as the platform provider. OpenAI's handling of that content is governed by the user's OpenAI account, plan, settings, and applicable OpenAI privacy terms.

## Retention

The developer does not retain copies of user content because these plugins do not send that content to developer-operated storage or services. Any retention by the ChatGPT/OpenAI platform is governed by OpenAI's applicable retention policies and the user's data controls.

## User controls

Users control which files and information they provide to the plugins and should provide only data necessary for the requested task. Platform-level chat, file, and data-management controls are provided by OpenAI. There is no separate developer account or developer-side data store for these plugins.

## Security and sensitive information

Users should avoid providing sensitive personal information that is not necessary for the requested workflow. The plugins are designed to process only the content needed for the task selected by the user.

## Changes

This policy may be updated if the plugins' data practices change. Material changes will be reflected in the published policy.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the public issue tracker at https://github.com/Nofates/AutoPlug-Client/issues.
